Biography

General information

Who he was

Steven Paul Jobs was born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco, California. He was a co-founder of Apple Inc. and became one of the most influential figures in technology and design.

Career highlights

Jobs helped launch products such as the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone, and iPad, while also leading Pixar to remarkable animation success.

Legacy

He believed deeply in simplicity, user experience, and pushing ideas beyond what was technically possible. His design philosophy still inspires product teams worldwide.

His creations

Products that changed the world

1976

A hand-built personal computer that helped establish Apple as a serious technology company.

1984

Macintosh

Introduced a new era of personal computing with a graphical user interface and mouse-based interaction.

1998

iMac

Brought a colorful, approachable design to the desktop computer and revived Apple’s identity.

2001

iPod

Reinvented portable music with a sleek interface and elegant design.

2007

iPhone

Combined communication, internet, and computing into one revolutionary device.

2010

iPad

Created a new category of computing: a tablet designed for touch-first interaction.
